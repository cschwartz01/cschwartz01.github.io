Am I Human?

Am I Human? is a multimedia artwork created for a Junior Seminar class at Allegheny College in 2023. It is is a collection of collages that explore the word ‘human’ and how it is defined within our society. It begs the question, “What is human?” and asks the viewer to consider what exactly makes us human and why this is the case. Constructed from pre-existing materials, these collages mirror the process of finding oneself in a pre-existing world. This piece encourages reflection on the relationship between the self and the external world, considering how our identities are shaped and discovered in the context of a world that predates our individual existence. Just as these collages are lots of little pieces put together to create something unique, so are we.
