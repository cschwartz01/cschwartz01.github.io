My name is Charlie Schwartz and I am a multimedia artist and filmmaker.

About Me

As a mixed-media artist, I am continually exploring the intricacies of human existence and perception of the world around us through my work. My practice is deeply rooted in the exploration of communication and connection, as I seek to understand how our interactions with the world shape who we become and how we view ourselves and others. I find inspiration in the mundane and overlooked—the pieces of our lives that tend to go unnoticed or even discarded. By bringing attention to these elements, I aim to prompt viewers to reconsider their own perceptions of the world around them and introduce a new perspective. In addition to my visual art, I am a filmmaker. While my films often resonate with the themes present in my artwork, they exist as separate yet complimentary expressions of my artistic narrative, a recognition of the many different ways to communicate. At the heart of much of my work is a focus on communication and connection and an inquiry into the essence of humanity.
